Have you ever heard of Vietnamese cuisine?
If not, that would be a great loss in your life.
Vietnamese cuisine is incredibly diverse and reflects
a rich culinary culture, combining salty, sweet, sour, spicy flavors and family recipes
to create unforgettable tastes.
Below are some popular breakfast dishes in Vietnam that are favored by many people because they are inexpensive and easy to eat.
The most popular dishes are Pho and Banh Mi.
Pho is for those who like hot food with soup, while Banh Mi is more convenient and is loved by many young people.
